## 2. Core Research Questions (For Report & Presentation)

1. **Growth Trajectory:** How rapidly did Uzbekistan's IT services exports and IT Park resident ecosystem expand between 2017 and 2026?
2. **Structural Shift:** How has the share of ICT services in Uzbekistan's total service exports (`BX.GSR.CCIS.ZS`) and GDP evolved post-2020?
3. **Regional Benchmark:** How does Uzbekistan's ICT export growth compare against CIS peers (Kazakhstan, Armenia, Georgia, Belarus)?
4. **2030 Strategic Feasibility:** Based on historical trends and machine learning projections, what growth rate is required to reach the *Digital Uzbekistan 2030* target of **$5 billion** in annual IT exports?
